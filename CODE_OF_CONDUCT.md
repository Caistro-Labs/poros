# Code of Conduct

Poros follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, technical, and respectful.
- Assume good intent, but do not tolerate harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on Poros correctness, usability, performance, and documentation.
- Be especially careful with benchmark claims: numbers must come from measured runs.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers at contact@caistrolabs.com. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable.
